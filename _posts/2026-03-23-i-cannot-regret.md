_The bloodstains sullying amidst the shouts_

It came in drips and drops, then stains. I didn't notice until I was screamed about it. 

'How could I have not noticed? Do you know how much it takes to clean it? Why didn't you pay attention?'

I had been playing games, immersed by the Internet to pay little attention. In ways, such absorption to ignore such problems naturally happening and naturally felt would be my fault. 

It was easy to cry over the spilt milk, the shouts about it even when I went all the way upstairs to clean my soiled clothes with the screams still following me all the way up. The tears came unshed, even when I just scrubbed the blood away.

It was easy to wish if I had paid more attention, if I could avoid a scolding and I truly felt. But I also numbed myself.

Shouting doesn't do away soiled clothes and sullied seats. It does not do away what naturally happens or simply the poor timing. It does not rouse alertness to the unpredictability.

You cannot shout at nature. You cannot shout at a done deal. You cannot shout at the results.

Because they are already done. The only thing left is to do.

Thus numbing myself, with the acceptance that it already happened. And made the shouts easier to endure. Easier to ignore. Easier to know that the unchangeable is not going to suddenly change by that force of will, by that complaint. What's left is to grit teeth and settle the aftermath.

_The torn wings, at flight out of the gilded cage_

No one ever knows what the future has in store, and most let actions dictate to what was known best in present. Foresight to the future is left to those with more experience, politically and business inclined and those living with all the youth in the world pretending that it never otherwise affects as long as dreams remain achievable.

I do not deny its naivete, that only happens looking back. It reminds me about the shouts in the aftermath. It's the shouts that I hear once more, in an entirely different situation than letting out blood. Still similar when I am letting out blood in a more metaphorical sense, giving my life out trying to livelihood. Believing I can hide through youthful years to grow up later.

It never occured to me that escape from a dependent life to somewhere entirely new as fearful and wondrous will pay its debt, without achievable dreams when permanancy without the right skillset, experience and documentation is a risk. When getting the skillset, experience and documentation requires a permancy that cannot be otherwise gotten.

It felt like years wasted, enjoying temporary youth when all that youth must be poured into experiences in order to keep building up a life.

But I accept my tomfoolery because in a life so dependent, it was something I cannot obtain freely. And yet tomfoolery may cost me my continued freedom with how truly clipped I am figuring where I went wrong, why I can't dream and why it's so hard to pursue what I want.

Everyone says life is not easy as it is what it is. I ask, why do we have to suffer for it? And it's only through spite that I can only go through with it, because there is nothing left to do.

_The compliance over confrontation_

It was easier to let them scream than scream back. It was easier to let honeyed words drip over my ears than wonder what poison was sprinkled all over. It was easier to go along the ride for opportunities than shun it altogether with the what-if just dangling in front of my eyes.

Even then, there are limits. From scams, from experience needing to be paid over earned. From events promising so much and then leaving more to be desired. From people who I have no choice to trust and give as much and smile even when it falls so short because people are still human, with their own limitations needing to focus on themselves.

It's something I cannot hate. It's something I don't even consider whether I like.

'How can you still respect and trust someone despite all that?'

What else, when I got into what I have given? What else, when there were still good things over the bad?

What else, when I had experienced, doubled over in pain and have to get back up as long as I am alive?

I had enough self-respect to stop myself from falling further into the abyss. And yet, I cannot stop those experiences shaping me to who I am, to weigh each and everyone with their own benefits and harm. And to keep going just for the glimmering possibility that maybe, just maybe it could be my big breakthrough.

What else, when there is little choice?

To once more grit my teeth in the aftermath when shouting changes nothing?

_Distancing from shards once taken to never let go_

Discomfort is a sign in moving forward.

That stops, when discomfort overwhelms to tears.

'It happens for a reason. When something happens, it just happens and at least it's not worse. I can accept it. It's fine.'

Is it, pushing myself to no return? Is it, pushing myself to the abyss? Is it, when all that pushing just balks me backwards and stops me to my knees?

When, I have always wondered, that we had to conform to everything even as we ignore our own indivudal wants, needs, likes and dislikes just to live instead of living our own lives?

Life is not easy, would be the answer.

That shouldn't make living itself to keep on doing everything and not get rewarded in reasonable ways, to the light at the end of the tunnel that makes sense, to keep holding on and expecting a job well done for facing hardships that bring people down their knees with tears in the eyes.

It makes it easy to let go. Let go of parents' expectations, let go of extra classes, let go of piano. Let go of old ways to get jobs, let go of the number games, let go of people with surface level advice and no response later.

Maybe I will regret what I miss, when I see people thrive from it but I didn't thrive no matter how hard I try. And so why do I have to cling on things that made me tired? I feel judgement in the eyes of those that thrive, and like those shouts, I cannot take back my decisions. Just not look back.

_The friends let go from emotion_

We cannot live without hurting.

We cannot live without connecting and breaking.

Relationships of all shapes and sizes have come and go.

Even worse than the shouts, the expectations and disappointment.

And yet, what was let go just meant that the journey together reached its end and more can be gained by making our own paths.

Even with insidious whispers about sticking on. The trust clung to, the need to be in each other's arms.

At least by now, I have lived enough that I know I don't live by others. Just in stages, but I do not need people in my life to live my own.

_Clinging to cliches and to prove_

It is an old generation bias, and a new one's slowness to adapt.

It is belief of one size fits all, and that nothing is original anymore sticking to old tropes.

Everytime I apply for a job. Everytime I fear for interviews yet to come. Everytime family says 'because we are family' or religion that says 'Not by your own power but God's' or friends say 'as long as you preserve.'

And I search, relentlessly, for anything that will work for me. To manifest, to delude. To close my eyes and hope. 

To be chaotic, to will myself to violence and insanity 'for the plot' when nothing else will. And still clinging, to anything that will make life better.

Or to spitefully prove I didn't need them when it doesn't.

And find out that everything I do... what I cannot take back, even as I continue on...

When I hear that desparation, I shut down. Because it ties closely with regret, and ever since the shouts, the unchangeable aftermath, desperation numbs itself to willful acceptannce. Because I cannot change what I did and I don't want desperation closely tied to regret.

Because what use is regret to the unchangeable?

_The edge between the sky and the sea_

And what use is regret in death?

If there is one thing the numbness to aftermath taught me, about how life is not easy, is that what happens cannot be cursed upon, lamented because it has less to do with the individual and more that it just happens.

Funny then that no one likes it when I say 'If I die today, it's just meant to be.'

Which really is fine by me, because who exactly can regret death when people can't even feel? We can't choose how to die, but sometimes we wonder whether we are closer to the edge than we think. Living and moving closer to the grave, trying to accomplish something without success.

Maybe that's why I think about the ways to die, about keeping a farce of a will even when there's no legal attachment to it. 

Maybe that's why I like falling, the idea of skydiving because there's nothing except for the rush of the sky, the thrill of life and the firmness of ground. And I think about distance between the sky and sea. To fall with air and water before hitting ground, in a long drop between those two.

I won't say, not here, when I don't want people to think about how to die. I wonder whether it can be thought about how to live. With the limitless possibilities in the skies and the plunge to the sea bellow to swim back to surface and then look up at sky with its beautiful air.

It makes it seem inevitable, even as we skim to those edges, that life has constantly push, pushed and kept pushing. To show up, to exist, to keep moving.

Never knowing when to hide, to be, to stop.

At least with falling there is little choice. Because once over the edge, all the shouting is not going to bring me back up.

To only accept, when there is nothing left for it.
